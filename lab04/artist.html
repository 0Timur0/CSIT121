My Favorite Music Artist: Tame Impala

My favorite music artist is Tame Impala, the psychedelic music project led by Australian multi-instrumentalist Kevin Parker. Tame Impala's music is known for being dreamy, relaxing, and groovy, often blending elements of rock, pop, and electronic music. My favorite song from Tame Impala is Let it happen.

Why Did I use this Image

I chose this image because it is a popular image of the lead singer Kevin Parker. The image only shows the singer himself but the image still includes some vibrant colors reflecting off himself. The image type I chose was a JPEG file because of its smaller file size and the image being a simple photograph.
